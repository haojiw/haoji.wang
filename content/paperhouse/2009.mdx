<Note>When I was in first grade, my mom bought me a diary. She said I could write anything I wanted in it. I always loved pretty notebooks so I was happy. That was when I started writing.</Note>

---

<Scan src="/images/2009/diary-lexus.jpg" alt="Diary entry: the car show" tilt="left" />

今天，我和爸爸妈妈一起去汽车博览会，那里有各种各样的汽车。我看中了一辆雷克萨斯。

Translation: Today I went to a car show with mom and dad. There were all kinds of cars. I had my eyes on a Lexus.

<Note>Ok total coincidence, I drive a Lexus today, my first car. This feels like breaking the 4th wall LOL! I drive a 2005 LS430 and my goal is to take care of it for 10 more years.</Note>

---

<Scan src="/images/2009/diary-supermarket.jpg" alt="Diary entry: the supermarket" tilt="right" />

今天，我和妈妈去自选商场里买东西。我认识了各种各样的食品，比如：双汇王中王，太太乐鸡精，成长快乐。今天我很开心，因为去了这么漂亮的自选商场。比以前去的自选商场更开心！

Translation: Today, me and mom went to a supermarket to buy stuff. I learned the names of all kinds of foods, like (sausage brand), (chicken bouillon brand), and (vitamins brand). I'm very happy today, because we went to such a pretty supermarket. Happier than the supermarkets we've been to before!

---

<Scan src="/images/2009/diary-sun.jpg" alt="Diary entry: the pale blue sun" tilt="left" />

我画了一个淡蓝的太阳，送给马路。淡蓝的太阳吸收了废气，以后空气变得清爽、新鲜了。

Translation: I painted a pale blue sun, and I gifted it to the road. The pale blue sun absorbed all the smogs. From then on, the air became fresh and clean.

---

<Scan src="/images/2009/diary-autumn.jpg" alt="Diary entry: autumn" tilt="right" />

满地的枫叶像金色的地毯一样，他们在风的指挥下，飞了起来。<br />
他们边飞边喊：“秋天来啦！秋天来啦！”<br />
苹果好奇地问：“秋天在哪里，秋天在哪里？”<br />
苹果不知道自己就是秋天。

<Note>看来写日记一年之后，我学会了很多字，这篇都不用拼音了。很棒！</Note>

Translation:

Maple leaves blanketed the ground like a golden carpet.<br />
They took flight, under the wind's direction.<br />
As they flew, they called out: "Autumn is here! Autumn is here!"<br />
An apple asked curiously, "Where is autumn? Where is autumn?"<br />
The apple never thought to look at itself. The apple is autumn.
