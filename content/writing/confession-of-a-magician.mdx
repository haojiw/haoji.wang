### Magician's Confession: 

Language. This deck of cards I manipulate with my hands. Since young I have known it has power. I speak to others and I see faces change. I speak to a mirror and walk out in a new skin.

Language. The only material given to us to build the Tower of Babel. I have lived and I have seen its power. The things we say program the heart and soul, in all different shapes where world becomes colorful, and we form tribes of people. I walk through this earth and see thousands of lives, often feel compelled enough to chameleon into a few. 

Language. Synonymous with knowledge. The wild horse, the serpent, or the femme fatale I have chased and finally tamed. Do you know, I've spent decades fantasizing what it'd be like if only you were mine; now you sleep soundly by my side. I spend night and day with her, they refer to us as "Athena and the Wise." My life before her has become a distant mirage; until tonight she whispered to me: "where would you be, without me?" 

Where would I be? How would I be? The echoing sound start driving me crazy. Gentlemen, don't you see the irony? Language; only way for me to understand the absence of her, is through her. Language, my stunning mistress, devil in a new dress, whispering in my ear asking what I'd do without her, while undressing right in front of my eyes. She has crawled near me, I feel my body succumbing to the familiar scent of danger. Yes, no. Fuck. I'm forced to face my love and lust for you. I've known since the first time I met you, that you are my light and my doom. 

Forgive me God, for I've failed to overcome my obsession. My pathological obsession, with the picture instead of the truth. But you know it already, don't you? You've plotted this as my great misfortune. I was more complete without her. I was a kid without her. Before I met her, I spent years in unconsciousness. Never looked within myself, never analyzed this life. I yearn for that time, back then I know nothing, and all I know was peace. 

At this point, I could no longer hypothesize a life where I never met her. Father, you've planned this encounter. I know, yes you told me in a dream: you don't give me battles that I am not ready for; but father, can I say I am confused? How do you unlearn things, erase the paper all brand new? Father I am sorry, sorry for trying in arrogance, sorry for putting my own glory before yours, believing I can handle all this without your help. All this effort to build a better self, word by word, knowledge by knowledge, skin by skin, I overlooked this great irony: I never needed more. The heart and soul I was born with were already complete. The most complete and the most vulnerable. So delicate and vulnerable, I feel insufficient in front of her allure. As I put on armors out of insecurity, the heart lose its tender; the armor was carved out from the tenderness lost, leaving scars that I no longer have strength to heal. 

### Father's Response: 

You know of yourself as a human. Your little species, born in the Garden of Eden, was given the best of all things. Until your ancestor's courtesy towards one odd creature and appetite for one strange fruit, decided you needed more, more than the best things. Stretching their feeble arms to seek strength from Satan; O the arms were feeble, yet why have you forgotten: I promised you, I promised to protect you. 

Yes I saw, in their heart I made with soft gold, a voice of silver chrome arose: "Humans, have you considered: you can protect yourself too?" I saw Adam nod in hypnosis: yes. With that nod the gates started closing behind him, thereby began your exile. 

But who says the heaven lost, is better than the towers you built? Millennials has passed, I've came to see my harshness too. Son, the past was in the past, the future is manifold. Go, venture in the ocean; go build, go live the life you've chose. One day you will all come back to my side, where you'd have the chance to tell me the wonders you saw. 

### Magician's Diary: 

Language. Bricks, to build the Tower of Babel; stones, also known as the Sisyphean rock. I saw a vision. It was very well defined, when he wrote the code for this matrix, that nothing made by our hands will ever reach his world. Our people came to know, the best thing that could be done and has been done, is to build such diversity of architectures that populates around earth; it is no Eden, but we feel safe enough to call it home. 

Today she came by and I said no. God has shown me again and again, tragedy begins when I strive to find myself in her. So much of the lessons I learned, is about unlearning, resisting, and recognizing no man-made tower is ultimately safe enough to shelter in. O God forbid, a man wants security; yet as he sleep in comfort the tower starts to crumble. He had no choice but fled, and was sent back dwelling on the great wasteland. There he must sit alone and grieve. There among the rubbles he was told, one must build a house with his own hands -- thus even if the house crushes, he leaves no regrets; for he's been gifted the highest reward, a dignified call back to heaven. The badge of honor, to stand and die for what he built, and what he chose. 

To the gentlemen reading this, let us remember, do not seek solace in false Gods: rationalism, nationalism, communism, transhumanism; great individuals, great pleasures, the universe, stories of love. Those are architectures made for men in lethargy, to keep them in lethargy. Great numbers of such men make the tower tumble. Remember,  it is a sin to reside in a home not meant for you; you must build your own. The only solace you will find, is up in the sky, when you miss where we came from. 

Remember, do not build things to flatter him either, for the mind of God has no preference. No this or that, see things as is. He has instructions for you. Build, as you were told. 

As my diary concludes, the beauty is tamed and sleeping sound in my arms. Another day spent peacefully, until inevitably a storm interrupts this calm ocean. Then I know, once again, it is time to look up and seek strength from the Father above.
